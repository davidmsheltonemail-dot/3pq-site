01The three laws

3PQ begins by recognizing three laws.

They are observed, not invented — the conditions under which a commitment between parties holds or fails. 3PQ is their application to how owners buy complex capital projects.

“Between two, a promise hangs by a common measure, not individual will.”

The governing axiom

Law I

Division of Common Measure

One domain owns the ask and the other owns the answer.

Law II

Conservation of Accountability

Divided authorship does not divide the promise. It strips the address off it, and the unaddressed share falls to the party that cannot walk away from the asset.

Law III

Limit of Restraint

A promise holds only to the limit both parties can bear to stay in their own domains.

The owner's warrant

Define and warrant the problem, stay out of the answer — and the builder warrants the solution.

The warranty is the owner's reward for restraint. Reach across the gate into means, methods, or design and you co-author the answer: the warranty voids, and the unaddressed share falls back to the owner, the one who cannot walk away from the asset. Stay in your domain and you hold a warranty; reach into theirs and you spend it.

02What 3PQ is

Define what a facility must do.
Hold the builder to proof.

3PQ is a structured method for owners who buy complex capital projects through design-build — a single contract for both design and construction. Rather than prescribing how a facility must be built, the owner defines what it must do, in prioritized and measurable terms, and holds the design-builder accountable to those terms through documented proof.

The name states the structure. Three requirement domains — each a P — define the project. A fourth discipline — Q — verifies that what was built matches what was required. Requirements are ranked as Required, Desired, or If Possible, so the design-builder solves to the owner’s true priorities within a firm fixed price.

The 3PQ Acquisition & Management System is an owner-side design-build procurement methodology for capital construction. It is unrelated to similarly named commercial software or configuration products.

03The framework — three P’s and a Q

Three domains define the project. One discipline verifies it.

PDomain 1

Procedures

Who does what for whom?

The roles and responsibilities of owner and design-builder — the rules of the competition, the contract, and the design and construction procedures that govern the work.

PDomain 2

Program

How much do I want?

The project’s requirements in metrics and capacities — spaces, functions, adjacencies, equipment, and the vision and objectives behind them.

PDomain 3

Performance Criteria

What level of quality do I need?

Measurable specifications for structure, exterior, interior, services, and site — each defined by function, comfort, safety, durability, and operations.

Q

Verification

Quality

Now track and assure the results.

Substantiation: documented verification that the delivered work complies with the three requirement domains — before, during, and after construction.

04Four phases, one accountable line

From the owner’s expectations to post-occupancy substantiation.

1

Project Definition

Capture and document the owner’s expectations, risks, budget and finance goals, operational needs, and a project scope and program brief.

2

Procurement Model Development

Analyze risk, budget, and operations; develop the detailed program and the performance criteria that will drive the solicitation.

3

RFQ / RFP Documentation — 3PQ

Author the solicitation: partnering procedures, program documents, performance specifications, and the evaluation and selection criteria.

4

Project Implementation

From pre-award through post-occupancy: contract development, compliance auditing, advisement and facilitation, commissioning, and substantiation.

05Proven in practice

Federal, military, university, and municipal owners.

U.S. Department of Energy · NREL (now National Laboratory of the Rockies) · Golden, CO

Research Support Facility

DOE’s Research Support Facility at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory was procured on the 3PQ Acquisition & Management System. Completed in 2011 as the nation’s largest net-zero-energy office building, it earned LEED® Platinum and was delivered on a firm fixed price and on schedule. Its net-zero data center received a 2011 GreenGov Presidential Award for Green Innovation from the White House. DOE’s published account documents the 3PQ Acquisition and Management System by name.

City of Liberty · Missouri

Liberty Wastewater Treatment Facility

An early performance-based design-build wastewater treatment facility in the United States, structured on 3PQ. Recognized with DBIA regional and national awards and EPA recognition for clean-water infrastructure.

Kansas State University · Olathe, KS

International Animal Health & Food Safety Institute

A $28 million facility delivered on budget with zero change orders — the quiet signature of a feasibility-closed procurement — and certified LEED Silver.

USACE · Army & Air National Guard

Federal & military design-build

3PQ has structured design-build procurements for the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers and for Army and Air National Guard facilities — among them the Arkansas Air National Guard A-10 maintenance facility for the 188th Fighter Wing at Fort Smith.
